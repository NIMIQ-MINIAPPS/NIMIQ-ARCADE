# NIM-ARCADE Pricing

## Free Tier (Default)
- Price: 0 NIM/month
- Features: All 30 games, XP earning, leaderboards, profile
- Limits: 50,000 XP/day earning cap

## Tournament Entry
- Daily tournament: 0.001–0.01 NIM per entry
- Weekly tournament: 0.01–0.05 NIM per entry
- Monthly tournament: 0.05–0.1 NIM per entry
- Prize distribution: 70% first / 20% second / 10% third
- Platform fee: 5% of pool

## Online Multiplayer Rooms
- Minimum entry: 0.001 NIM
- Maximum entry: 0.1 NIM
- Custom rooms: configurable 0.001–1 NIM
- Prize pool: sum of all entries minus 5% platform fee

## XP → NIM Conversion Rate
- 1,000 XP = 0.001 NIM
- Minimum conversion: 1,000 XP
- No conversion fee
- Processed instantly via Nimiq Pay

## Notes
- All prices in NIM (Nimiq native cryptocurrency)
- No fiat currency accepted
- No subscriptions or recurring charges
- All transactions processed through Nimiq Pay wallet
